- **ID**: `T10-01`
- **Judul**: Click-through Mini Player memblokir scroll lirik manual, klik-seek baris lirik, dan double-click ganti mode di area tengah
- **Lokasi**: `src-tauri/src/miniplayer_window.rs:318-330`, `src-tauri/src/miniplayer_window.rs:504-508`, `src/components/miniplayer/MiniPlayerCardMode.tsx:215`, `src/components/miniplayer/MiniPlayerCardMode.tsx:264`, `src/components/miniplayer/MiniPlayerCardMode.tsx:355`, `src/components/miniplayer/MiniPlayerBarMode.tsx:216`

  2. Arahkan kursor ke area daftar lirik di tengah kartu (mis. y=200px, di antara toolbar atas 52px dan progress bar bawah 22px).
